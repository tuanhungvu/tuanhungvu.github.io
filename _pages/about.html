--- layout: academic permalink: / title: "About" sitemap: true news_visible: 8 redirect_from: - /about/ - /about.html --- {%- assign p = site.data.profile -%} {%- capture bio -%} I am a Senior Research Scientist at [valeo.ai](https://www.valeo.com/en/valeo-ai/) (R&D Technical Manager, Valeo Expert) and a part-time researcher in the computer vision group [Astra-vision](https://astra-vision.github.io/) at Inria Paris. I received my PhD from [École Normale Supérieure](https://www.ens.fr/en) under the supervision of [Ivan Laptev](https://www.di.ens.fr/~laptev). Before that, I obtained an engineering degree from [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en) and, in parallel, a "Master 2" degree in Mathematics, Machine Learning and Computer Vision ([MVA](https://www.master-mva.com/)) from [École Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay](https://ens-paris-saclay.fr/en) in 2014. My research focuses on deep learning for scene understanding, with an emphasis on making perception models robust to distribution shift: domain adaptation and generalization, vision-language models, 3D perception, and generative models for autonomous driving. {%- endcapture -%}
